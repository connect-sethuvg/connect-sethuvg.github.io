Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the Daily Science Dose website and the "Daily Science Dose Uploader", a private tool the channel owner uses to publish videos to the Daily Science Dose YouTube channel through YouTube API Services.

What data the uploader uses

  • Google account authorization (OAuth tokens) of the channel owner only, stored on the owner's own computer.
  • The channel's own videos and metadata: video files, titles, descriptions, tags and visibility settings, used to upload and manage the channel's own videos.

The uploader does not access, collect or store any data about viewers or other YouTube users, and does not use any data for advertising.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, as required to upload and manage videos.

Storage and deletion

Authorization tokens are kept only on the owner's computer and can be deleted at any time. Access can also be revoked at Google Account → Security → Third-party access.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

This website is a static page. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical data such as IP addresses for security, under its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions: leave a comment on any video on the Daily Science Dose YouTube channel.
